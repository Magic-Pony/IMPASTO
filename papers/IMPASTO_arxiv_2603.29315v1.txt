Stochastic Sampling with CMA-Style Adaptive Covariance
   At each MPPI optimization iteration, we assemble a can-
didate set by inserting the current nominal sequence U and
drawing the remaining K−1 samples from a time-indexed
Gaussian. Concretely, for each time index t we generate
standard normal noise zt ∼ N (0, I), apply the current
covariance structure via a Cholesky factor At of Σt , where
Σt = At A⊤ t , and form perturbed controls

                     Ũt = Ut + σt At zt .                 (20)
The full sequences are clipped to action bounds. This yields
a batch of candidates centered at U but shaped by (σt , Σt )
at every timestep.
   From all K candidates, we select the top Ke by terminal
cost and compute temperature-weighted softmax weights
with temperature β over the elite set. Using those weights,
we update the nominal sequence via an exponential moving
average (EMA) toward the elite mean and perform CMA-
style per-timestep adaptation: the step-size path ps (t) updates
σt , and the covariance path pc (t) together with a rank-µ term
updates Σt .
